# Geist font assets

User-directed typography, November 2, 2026: Geist Serif Regular for headings and the wordmark; Geist Mono for body text, navigation, labels, and controls.

Both files are unmodified, self-hosted WOFF2 assets with their matching SIL OFL 1.1 licenses.

- `geist-serif-regular.woff2`: official `geist@1.9.0`, `dist/fonts/geist-serif/GeistSerif-Regular.woff2`. Upright Regular 400 with round-dot variant; not a variable-weight face.
